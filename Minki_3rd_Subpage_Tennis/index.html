SET 2 — THE JOURNEY

The reason why I love Tennis

Tenacity and Resilience

I love tennis because it combines all of the skills and qualities that I want to and enjoy developing in. Like playing the flute, tennis requires a lot of breath control and enduring. Managing my breath while doing any kind of activity helps me to maintain my concentration and manage my energy throughout the challenge. And I can perform my best with breath control under pressures. I personally do not like sports that requires contact with the other team members because it feels like they are taking away my energy and breaths as I continue to hit my body towards them or get injured. However, I rather prefer sports that requires mental thinking or cooperation with the team. Tennis is a sport that has both. It has single and doubles and it is also a combination of athletic movement and strategic thinking. That is why I find it both exciting and rewarding.

Podium, statue of recognition

people think that standing on the podium is the end, but it's actualy a new beginning

I’ve tried multiple games(more than ten) throughout my eighth grade varsity team at Fay and my last two were the games that I was the most recognized. For the first several games, it was all winning. Usually winning 6-1 or 6-0 and I thought this was the way to get recognized by the teammates and my coach. However, it was not only me that was winning. Winning became too common within our team. I never realized at the moment, but now that I think back about it, maybe what I pursued was recognition. For the last two games, it was a loss. For the second to last one, it was a double game with a teammate that I was not that close with, but we tried very hard throughout the game and even though we lost, we got more recognition after.

The last loss was more intense. I played with a junior student at Austin Prep and she actually made me try throughout the game. The game was very tense. One of the most tense games that I’ve ever had. 1–0, 1-1, 2-1, 2-2, 3-2. It kept on going like that, which made me want to win. We eventually got to the point where our scores were 6-6 and went to the tie breaker. I got really nervous at that point because too many people were watching outside the fence. However, the tie breaker went very bad. Maybe because I was sweating too much on my hands. It was 7-1. Terrine score. However, when the game was over, I felt more like winning, rather than a loss. It was the first ever game that I’ve actually tried and I was grateful that I got to experience that at the end of the season. When I stepped outside the court, that was when I got recognized by everyone. And that felt like a fresh new start for me, rather than only an end of the season.
