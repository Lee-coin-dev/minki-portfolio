Research Paper: Western Interior Sea

Abstract

During the Cretaceous period (143.1 to 66 MYA), there existed a vast seaway in what is now central North America for 34 million years. This seaway is called the Western Interior Seaway. However, the Western Interior Seaway gets struck by an asteroid, which is now called the K-Pg extinction because it caused a major worldwide extinction. The Western Interior Seaway experienced radical changes due to the K-pg extinction and we must be aware of the negative effects on our current ocean eco-system.

New Beginning

70% of the earth is covered by the ocean and it is the place where there are more lives than any other places on the earth. According to the BBC, the oceans are thought to have 2 million species, but only 250,000 of them are currently found. There are largely unexplored areas in the deep seas, such as the abyssal zone and there are thought to be scores of large marine animals there. The race is trying to explore the remaining species quickly for reasons, such as mining. However, they are hesitant to do that because the area that was untouched for millions of years might be too fragile to mine. Even if there are many issues, with the new methods of ocean exploration we are getting closer than ever to discovering more species.

99.999% of the deep ocean seafloor has never been directly explored by humans and this is the reason why I pursue marine biology more than any other areas of science. The ocean is the most unexplored place on earth because of many different reasons, such as extreme pressure, darkness, and high costs. However, I want to create advanced technology as I grow up, to explore more of the deep sea and make the 99.999% to 98%. Especially the fossils that will be embedded in the ocean floor as well. I believe that once I lower the percentage one day, the percentage will decrease rapidly. The current tools used are mapping tools or human occupied vehicles.
